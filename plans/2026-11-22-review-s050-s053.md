## Plan for "Fixes from s052 and s053" (review branch; nothing deployed)

1. **Repairs handle network changes.** `repair_user` shows each run of the test as proposed with its knobs and its network change. It gives the
   change format with one correct example built from the session's own brief (for again_again: remove the queue on link l1, then add a scheduler
   on l1). The example is checked against the brief before it is shown. The prompt says the change is kept unless the feedback is about it. When
   the harness refuses a change, its message ends with the same correct example. This replaces the interim sentence of the branch.
2. **Contested only if the test still tests its hypothesis.** The review of the last round asks one sentence, then a yes/no box: does this test
   still test the hypothesis it was made for? With "no", the test does not run, the hypothesis stays open for the next turn, and a note says why.
3. **Feedback names one problem.** The review asks for one specific problem, with the record ids and numbers it rests on, and what the repaired
   test must achieve, never the rule, runs or change (the existing cut of rules in feedback stays). The harness refuses a decision rule with more
   than 3 comparisons, giving that reason. This is the arm "supervised" only, as the sheet's heading says, unless the operator wants it everywhere.
4. **Speed.**
   - (a) One batched first review per turn: all new guesses (gate 0) and all tests (gate 1, round 1) of an answer in one supervisor call.
     Restatements, repairs, later rounds and follow-up questions stay one per item; tests no longer wait on guesses, since D (a refused guess is
     restated and never takes its hypotheses down).
   - (b) No supervisor call when the harness has already refused something. Every harness check is put before the call that would follow it, and
     each place is listed in the review notes.
   - (c) Short calls: the model server allows both a thinking budget (`thinking_token_budget`, enforced: the supervisor's calls stop at 4,499 of
     4,500) and thinking off (`enable_thinking: false`: 0.2 s, no reasoning). Built: repairs and restatements at 12,000 tokens (6,000 reasoning).
     Open for the operator: the follow-up question with thinking off, or with a budget of 1,000.
   - Expected, from step 0: guess and test reviews, 20.3 minutes in 24 calls, become about one call per turn with new work (about 6 calls of
     ~80 s, about 8 minutes); repairs, 20.3 minutes, become about 7 to 8 minutes at 6,000 reasoning tokens. Over the two sessions, about 64 → 40
     minutes of waiting (−35 to −40 %). The researcher's own turns (16.8 minutes) are unchanged.
5. **Units on the page.** `EV.numRows` (viewer/evidence_cards.js) compares a number in an agent's text with a measurement after converting both to
   the same unit (bps, kbps, Mbps, Gbps).
